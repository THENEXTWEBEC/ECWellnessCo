Privacy

This page explains what happens to information you share while browsing this site or contacting us. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Contact

If you write to us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive the information you choose to share to respond to your inquiry and, if requested, prepare a retreat proposal. You may ask about those messages or request their deletion by writing to Saenz.isabel@gmail.com.

External services

The WhatsApp button opens a Meta service and the email link opens your email application. Their own policies apply when you use those services. The typeface is served from this site.

Browsing

The site has no forms, user accounts or first-party analytics. The hosting provider may process technical logs needed to serve the page and keep it secure.

Images

Site photography consists of generated editorial representations illustrating the style of the experiences. The images do not document a specific retreat or accommodation.
